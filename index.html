Hot Hand Game

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 July 2026

This policy explains what data Hot Hand Game collects and how it is used. We do not ask you for personal details such as your name, email address, or phone number, and the app has no accounts or sign-in. Some data is nevertheless collected automatically to display advertising, as described below.

Advertising

The app displays advertising through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, AdMob may collect and process:

  • Device identifiers, including the advertising identifier (IDFA) where you have permitted tracking
  • Device and system information, such as model, operating system version, and language
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address
  • Interaction data, such as which ads were shown, viewed, or tapped

This processing is carried out by Google as an independent controller. Its handling of the data is governed by Google's own privacy policy, available at policies.google.com/privacy. Details on how Google uses data from apps that use its services are available at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Tracking and your choice

On first launch the app asks for your permission to track, as required by Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework. If you decline, personalised advertising is disabled and you will see non-personalised ads instead. You can change this at any time under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking on your device.

Data stored on your device

Game progress, scores, and settings are saved locally on your device. This data is not transmitted to us and is removed when you delete the app.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive or store your payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to those transactions.

Data we receive

We do not operate our own servers and receive no personal data about you directly. Aggregated, anonymised reports on ad performance and app downloads are available to us through Google AdMob and App Store Connect; these do not identify individual users.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided such information, contact us and we will address it.

Your rights

Under Swiss and European data protection law you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal data relating to you. For data processed by Google AdMob, requests are handled through Google using the links above. For anything within our control, contact us at the address below.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this page will be updated and the date above revised.
