Hot Hand Game

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 July 2026

This policy explains how Hot Hand Game handles your information. The short version: the app does not collect personal data.

Data we collect

Hot Hand Game does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or location.

Data stored on your device

Game progress, scores, and settings are saved locally on your device only. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you delete the app.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see or store your payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to those transactions.

Third parties

The app includes no analytics services, no advertising networks, and no third-party tracking. No data is shared with or sold to anyone.

Children

The app is safe for users of all ages. Because no data is collected, no data about children is collected either.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this page will be updated and the date above revised.
